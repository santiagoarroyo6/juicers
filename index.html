JUICERS Sydney, New South Wales
Battery operations

We keep shared e-bikes and e-scooters charged.

Juicers collects, charges and swaps batteries for shared micromobility fleets in Sydney, every day of the week, so the vehicles are ready when riders need them.

What we do

A small, trained field team and a charging site that runs seven days a week.

Battery swaps

Scheduled runs across the city to replace depleted batteries with fully charged ones.

Charging operations

Batteries are collected, charged and checked at our site before going back out.

Trained riders

Every rider on the team is trained in safe handling of batteries and vehicles.
